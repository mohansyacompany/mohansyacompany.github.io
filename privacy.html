Privacy Policy – ProRegister

MOHANSYA COMPANY · Effective date: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MOHANSYA COMPANY ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the ProRegister Android app ("the app"). By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: company ID, user ID, name, role, and the email address or phone number used for login and one-time password (OTP) verification.
  • Business data you enter: sites, staff, clients, workers, attendance, materials, stock, daily progress reports, tasks, sub-contractors, payments and expenses.
  • Photos, videos and documents that you choose to upload (for example task photos, material photos, chat media and agreement documents).
  • Chat messages sent inside the app's Work Chat.
  • Device and notification data: a push notification ID for your device, used only to deliver app notifications.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features and sync data between your company's users in real time.
  • To log you in securely and control access based on the permissions set by the company owner.
  • To send notifications about activity in your company (for example new entries, tasks or messages).
  • To provide support and fix problems.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your data

  • Data in a company is visible to that company's owner and to users the owner has given permission to.
  • Personal (one-to-one) chats are visible only to the two people in that chat.
  • We use the following service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf:
    • Google Firebase – database and authentication
    • Cloudflare (R2 and Workers) – storage of photos, videos and documents, and login services
    • OneSignal – push notifications
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Data retention

  • Task photos and videos, material photos and chat media are automatically deleted about 90 days after upload.
  • Agreement documents are kept while the project is active and are deleted 1 year after the project is closed.
  • Other business data is kept while the company account is active, or until it is deleted by the company owner.

5. Permissions used by the app

  • Camera – to take photos and videos for tasks, materials and chat.
  • Photos / media / files – to choose and upload existing photos, videos and documents.
  • Notifications – to show app notifications.
  • Internet – to sync data.

6. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to each company's data is restricted by login and database security rules. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Deleting your data or account

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing pkushawah44@gmail.com from your registered contact, with your company ID and user ID. We will process the request within 30 days. Company owners can also delete staff users and records from inside the app.

8. Children

The app is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated effective date.
