Delete your Mohansya Ledger account

Mohansya Ledger · by MOHANSYA COMPANY

Option 1: Delete from the app (instant)

  1. Open the Mohansya Ledger app and log in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Tap Account Delete, enter your Security Password (if set) and type DELETE.

Your account and all ledger data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to mohansyacompany@gmail.com from the same email address you use to log in, with the subject "Delete my Mohansya Ledger account".

We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email account in the app)
  • All ledger entries, opening balance and profile details (name, mobile number)
  • Your security password

Nothing is kept after deletion. PDF reports that you saved or shared earlier stay on the devices where you saved them.
