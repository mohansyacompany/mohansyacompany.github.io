Privacy Policy – Mohansya Ledger

MOHANSYA COMPANY · Effective date: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MOHANSYA COMPANY ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Mohansya Ledger Android app ("the app"). By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Email address – used to create your account and to send a one-time password (OTP) for login.
  • Ledger data you enter – entries (date, name, credit and debit amounts), opening balance and related notes.
  • Profile details you choose to add – name and mobile number shown on your reports.
  • Security password – stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, used to protect delete and balance changes.
  • Reminder time – stored only on your phone.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the ledger features and keep your data in sync and backed up.
  • To log you in securely with an email OTP.
  • To show a daily reminder notification, if you turn it on.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Where your data is stored and who can see it

  • Your ledger data is visible only to you, after you log in to your account.
  • We use these service providers to run the app:
    • Google Firebase – login (Authentication) and data storage (Realtime Database)
    • EmailJS – sending the login OTP email
  • PDF reports are created on your phone and are shared only when you choose to share them.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Data retention

Your data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, your ledger data and login are permanently deleted.

5. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: open Profile → Account Delete. Your login and all ledger data are deleted immediately and permanently.
  • Without the app: see Delete your Mohansya Ledger account.

6. Permissions used by the app

  • Internet – to log in and sync your data.
  • Notifications – to show the daily reminder (optional).
  • Run at startup – to set your reminder again after the phone restarts.

7. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and each account can read only its own data. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated effective date.
