About us

MOHANSYA COMPANY is a sole proprietorship business based in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. We build practical software tools for the Indian construction industry.

Our app: ProRegister

ProRegister helps construction company owners and their site staff manage day-to-day site work from one app:

  • Sites, staff and client access with role-based permissions
  • Worker attendance and labour records
  • Material entry and material stock
  • Daily Progress Reports (DPR)
  • Tasks with photos and videos
  • Sub-contractors, payments and extra expenses
  • Work chat between team members and notifications

ProRegister is available on Google Play.
